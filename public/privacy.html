Mintmark

Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

Mintmark appraises coins from photographs. This page describes exactly what happens to your data, in plain language.

What Mintmark collects

  • The photograph you take or choose, and an optional note you type about the coin.
  • The appraisal that comes back: identification, grade, value range and the reasoning behind it.
  • An anonymous id that the app generates on your device, used only to keep your collection separate from everyone else's.

What Mintmark never collects

  • No name, email address, phone number, or account of any kind — there is no sign-up.
  • No location data.
  • No advertising identifier, and no tracking of you across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Where your data goes

Your photograph is sent over HTTPS to the Mintmark gateway, which passes it to a vision model to produce the appraisal. Photographs and appraisals are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database so that your collection is still there the next time you open the app. Both are processed on our behalf; neither is sold, shared with advertisers, or used to build a profile of you.

How long it is kept

Until you delete it. Delete all appraisals in the app's Settings removes every saved coin, photo and appraisal for your device immediately, and deleting a single coin removes its photo with it.

Children

Mintmark is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Estimates, not appraisals in the professional sense

Values are estimates from a single photograph. They are not a professional grading service, a guarantee of value, or an offer to buy or sell a coin.

Questions about this policy?

Email support@innotel.us, or see support.
