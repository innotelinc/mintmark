Support

Help with appraisals, limits and your data.

How do I appraise a coin?

Open the app and tap Take a picture or Choose a photo. Fill the frame with one coin, front side up, in daylight or bright lamplight, on a plain surface. The appraisal takes about ten seconds.

Why was my photo rejected?

Mintmark needs to see the design, the date and the mint mark. It reads best when:

  • one coin fills the frame, with nothing covering it;
  • the light is even — flash glare and hard shadows hide detail;
  • the photo is sharp enough that lettering is legible.

Why is the value a range?

A photograph cannot show the reverse, the edge, or the coin in hand. The range is what a dealer might pay at the low end and a fair retail price at the high end, for the grade the photo shows. Worn common coins stay worth cents; cleaned or damaged coins are worth bullion or less.

“Mintmark has used up its appraisals for today”

Each device gets 25 appraisals a day, counted from midnight UTC. The whole service also has a daily ceiling, so on a busy day appraisals can stop for everyone until the counter resets. Settings shows both numbers.

Where is my collection stored?

On the Mintmark server, keyed to an anonymous id that this device keeps. There is no account to sign into, so the collection belongs to the device rather than to a login.

How do I delete my data?

In Settings, Delete all appraisals removes every saved coin, photo and appraisal. Individual coins can be deleted from their appraisal screen.

The app says it cannot reach the server

Appraisals need an internet connection; the model runs on the server and the app never holds an API key. If the address is wrong, open Settings → Gateway key and server and enter the server address.

Still stuck?

Email support@innotel.us with the app version, your device model, and what you saw.
